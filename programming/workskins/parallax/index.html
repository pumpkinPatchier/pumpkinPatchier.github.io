Parallax Demo

pumpkinPatchier

Summary:

Originally hosted on AO3. All the code, artwork, and formatting is created by me.

This piece was originally created for the 2026 AO3 HTML/CSS jam. I have anonymized the piece by 'Lorem Ipsum'ing it, but usually stands as an interactive piece where parts of the story are revealed by hovering your cursor over the visual components.

The main showcase here is the unconventional use of proportional units and positioning of images such that the pacing of the words match the pacing of the images regardless of the screen size. If you resize your window, you can observe the text shift around the images, until it falls either above or below it. This is usually made trivial using media-sensitive style sheets or the calc function, but neither of those are useable in AO3's code. Not only is the alignment respecting text important, but ensuring that the parallax effect lined up as intended on different displays was another challenge.

This piece was originally very tragic, but the words have since lost their meaning post-encryption. Still, while not explicit, the accompanying visuals may be disturbing to some audiences. Proceed with caution.
